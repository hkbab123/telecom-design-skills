# Concept Stage — Transmission / Fibre Backbone

Consultant-led feasibility, ahead of tender. Order-of-magnitude/parametric detail only.

1. Client hires **Consultant** for feasibility.
2. Consultant runs an **initial survey and feasibility study**: the alignment (rail corridor or pipeline/plant layout), existing fibre/duct assets, civil-works constraints, right-of-way and third-party fibre options (lease vs build), and the list of subsystems that will ride on the transport.
3. **Stakeholder requirements** gathered from operations, signalling/control, security, IT/cybersecurity, maintenance and HSE: required services per subsystem, availability expectations, growth horizon, legacy TDM interfaces (Q1b), hazardous-area/offshore sections (Q1c).
4. **Market RFI** to transmission/optical OEMs and fibre-cable suppliers: high-level technology options (SDH/NG-SDH, packet transport, OTN/DWDM), indicative node counts, budgetary pricing, lead times.
5. **Technology direction** presented as options (not a final choice): legacy-TDM-bearing vs all-packet, single-layer vs layered optical/packet, owned vs leased fibre.
6. **Route and diversity options** presented: single route with ring closure on the same corridor vs genuinely separate physical routes, with the SRLG exposure of each (`fundamentals/07-protection-restoration-physical-diversity.md`).

- **Calculations:** order-of-magnitude route length and node count; fibre-count estimate (working + spare + other-owner reserve); aggregate capacity per ring/segment from the service matrix (`fundamentals/09-capacity-dimensioning-service-mapping.md`, parametric accuracy only); node spacing against typical optical reach (`fundamentals/03-optical-link-budget-dispersion.md`, rough check only, not a real budget).
- **Deliverables:** feasibility study report, stakeholder requirements register, initial high-level proposal with technology/route options, budgetary price estimate, project approval package.
- **Roles:** Consultant produces; Client requests and approves; OEM/Vendor responds to RFI; no Contractor yet.
