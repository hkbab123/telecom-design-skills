# Concept Stage — FRMCS

Consultant-led feasibility, ahead of tender. Order-of-magnitude/parametric detail only.

1. Client hires **Consultant** for feasibility.
2. Consultant runs an **initial survey and feasibility study**: the line(s) and traffic types (high-speed, mainline, regional, metro), existing radio (GSM-R or other), fleet inventory and on-board readiness, tunnels/stations/viaducts, existing sites, towers and fibre assets, and the signalling roadmap (ETCS, ATO).
3. **Stakeholder requirements** gathered from operations, signalling, rolling stock, maintenance, security and IT/cybersecurity, passenger services, and safety/regulatory: application list with KPIs (`fundamentals/02-application-categories-qos-kpis.md`), availability expectations, growth horizon, cross-border and shared-infrastructure needs (Q1e).
4. **Transition strategy** established with the client (Q1b): greenfield, overlay or migration; the client's own timeline for GSM-R end-of-life; what happens to mixed fleets (`fundamentals/13-gsmr-coexistence-and-migration.md`).
5. **Spectrum position** confirmed (Q1d): which bands, how much, and who holds them; national regulator status.
6. **Delivery-model options** presented (Q1c), not a final choice: dedicated, hybrid, MNO-hosted/sliced, each with risk, control, cost and timeline (`fundamentals/10-delivery-models-private-hybrid-mno.md`).
7. **Market RFI** to FRMCS/5G OEMs, on-board gateway suppliers and (if relevant) MNOs: availability of products against the pinned standards baseline, indicative site counts, budgetary pricing, lead times, roadmap commitments.
8. **Standards baseline proposed** (UIC FRMCS release, 3GPP release) with the risk if it moves (`fundamentals/20-standards-maturity-and-version-control.md`).

- **Calculations:** order-of-magnitude site count for the chosen band against the corridor length (`fundamentals/06-coverage-link-budget-and-site-planning.md`, parametric only); rough capacity check of the largest-demand scenario against one carrier (`fundamentals/07-capacity-dimensioning.md`); fleet count needing on-board gateways.
- **Deliverables:** feasibility study report, stakeholder requirements register, transition strategy note, delivery-model options paper, initial high-level proposal, budgetary price estimate, project approval package.
- **Roles:** Consultant produces; Client requests and approves; OEM/Vendor/MNO responds to RFI; no Contractor yet.
