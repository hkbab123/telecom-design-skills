# Concept Stage — CCTV / Video Surveillance System

Consultant-led feasibility, ahead of tender. Order-of-magnitude/parametric detail only.

1. Client hires a **Consultant** for feasibility.
2. Consultant runs the **initial survey and feasibility study** — for rail, identifying which areas need coverage (platforms, concourses, tunnels, depots/yards, train interiors); for O&G, identifying perimeter, process-area, and access-control-adjacent coverage needs, and whether any camera will sit in a classified hazardous zone.
3. Consultant **collects requirements from stakeholders** — security/operations, safety, law-enforcement/evidentiary requirements (rail), HSE (O&G), IT (network/cybersecurity requirements), the regulator.
4. Consultant **floats queries to the market** (RFI-style, to camera/VMS OEMs and system integrators) for **initial budgetary estimations**.
5. Consultant prepares an **initial high-level proposal**, including a parametric camera-count estimate per coverage zone and VMS/storage architecture options.
6. Proposal, with pricing, goes into the **main project package** submitted for approval.

- **Inputs:** deployment context (rail station/platform/tunnel/train-borne vs O&G plant/perimeter); preliminary coverage-zone list; hazardous-area classification, preliminary (O&G); greenfield vs brownfield (existing analogue/legacy CCTV to replace or integrate); stakeholder requirements; applicable standards; retention-period expectation (regulatory or client policy); list of OEMs/platforms to approach.
- **Decisions:** which OEMs/VMS platforms to approach; which standards apply; centralised vs distributed VMS direction (if the client is open to discussing it this early); which coverage-zone options to present.
- **Calculations:** order-of-magnitude camera-count estimate per coverage zone (based on area/geometry and typical field-of-view coverage); order-of-magnitude storage estimate (see `fundamentals/04-bandwidth-storage-calculations.md` for the worked method, applied here only at parametric accuracy).
- **Deliverables:** feasibility study report, stakeholder requirements register, initial high-level proposal with camera-count/coverage options, budgetary price estimate, project approval package.
- **Roles:** Consultant produces; Client requests and approves; OEM/Vendor responds to RFI; no Contractor yet.
