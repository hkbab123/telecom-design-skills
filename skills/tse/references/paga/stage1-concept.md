# Stage 1 — Concept (project inception, pre-tender)

A client has an idea for a rail station/yard/depot public address and evacuation-alarm system, or an oil & gas plant/offshore PAGA system, and needs a feasibility study. **Consultant** is the primary actor at this stage; no Contractor engaged yet.

## Sequence

1. Client hires a **Consultant** for feasibility.
2. Consultant runs the **initial survey and feasibility study** — for O&G, this includes an early hazardous-area classification review (which zones exist, even before detailed equipment planning) and identifying which safety systems PAGA will need to integrate with (Fire & Gas, ESD); for rail, an early review of which areas need public-address coverage (platforms, concourse, yards, depots) and whether an existing passenger information system (PIS) exists to interface with.
3. Consultant **collects requirements from stakeholders** — operations, safety/HSE (critical for the General Alarm function), fire engineering, other client departments, the regulator/fire authority — and identifies which standards apply.
4. Consultant **floats queries to the market** (RFI-style, to PAGA OEMs/system integrators) for **initial budgetary estimations**.
5. Consultant prepares an **initial high-level proposal**, including **options across different OEMs** (named generically — never with invented specific figures) and a high-level distinction between the PA-only scope and the safety-rated GA scope.
6. Proposal, with the **price estimation**, goes into the **main project package** submitted for approval.

## Inputs

- Deployment context (rail station/platform/yard/depot vs O&G plant/topside/offshore) and site basics (area, number of buildings/zones, approximate headcount/occupancy — order of magnitude)
- Hazardous-area classification, at least preliminary, for O&G (any Zone 0/1/2 or Division 1/2 areas expected?)
- Whether General Alarm is a genuinely safety-critical function here (almost always yes for O&G process facilities; confirm for rail — GA on a station is typically fire-evacuation-driven, not process-safety-driven)
- Existing systems to interface with: Fire Alarm Control Panel, Fire & Gas detection, ESD, PABX/telephone paging, CCTV, passenger information system (rail)
- Greenfield vs brownfield (existing PA/tannoy or GA system present needing replacement or extension?)
- Stakeholder requirements: operations, HSE/safety, fire engineering, other client departments, regulatory/fire authority
- Applicable standards identified for the project/region, incl. ATEX/IECEx if hazardous areas are present
- Country/region (fire code and electrical/hazardous-area regulation are jurisdiction-specific)

## Decisions

- Which OEMs to approach for RFI
- Which standards apply (voice-alarm standard, fire code, functional-safety framework)
- Whether the General Alarm function will need a formal SIL rating (flag as a Stage 2 item to resolve with the client's HSE function, not decided here)
- Which OEM options to present

## Calculations

Order-of-magnitude/parametric only — approximate zone count and loudspeaker count from floor area/occupancy, not a detailed acoustic model or amplifier sizing calculation yet.

## Deliverables

- Feasibility study report (including preliminary hazardous-area review for O&G, and an early PA/GA scope split)
- Stakeholder requirements register
- Initial high-level proposal with OEM options
- Budgetary price estimate
- Project approval package

## Roles

- **Consultant** — produces all deliverables at this stage
- **Client** — requester and final approver of the package
- **OEM/Vendor** — responds to market queries with budgetary data
- **Contractor** — not engaged yet
