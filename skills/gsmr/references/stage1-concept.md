# Stage 1 — Concept (project inception, pre-tender)

The client has an idea for a rail or oil & gas project that needs a GSM-R network. The **Consultant** is the primary actor at this stage, hired by the client to turn the idea into a fundable proposal. No Contractor is engaged yet — that happens after this stage.

## Sequence

1. Client hires a **Consultant** for feasibility.
2. Consultant runs the **initial survey and feasibility study**.
3. Consultant **collects requirements from stakeholders** — operations, other client departments, the regulator — and identifies which standards apply.
4. Consultant **floats queries to the market** (RFI-style, to OEMs/vendors) to get **initial budgetary estimations**, so the client can set the budget for the EPC package.
5. Before going to market, the Consultant prepares an **initial high-level proposal**, including **options across different OEMs**.
6. This proposal, together with the **price estimation**, goes into the **main project package** submitted for approval — to government, client leadership, or an investor, depending on who is funding it.

## Inputs

- Project type (new line, upgrade, metro, mainline, freight) and route/site basics (length, stations, tunnels, depots — order of magnitude)
- Greenfield vs brownfield (existing GSM-R/telecom infrastructure present?)
- Stakeholder requirements: operations, other client departments, regulatory bodies
- Applicable standards identified for this project/region
- Country/region (spectrum band — R-GSM 900 MHz allocation, regulatory authority)
- Single-country or cross-border (if cross-border: which countries — note this only flags the core-placement decision for Stage 2, it isn't decided here)
- List of OEMs to approach for the market RFI, and what to ask them for (budgetary estimate basis)
- Who the proposal and price estimate ultimately go to for approval (government / client leadership / investor) — affects how formal/detailed the proposal needs to be

## Decisions

Which OEMs to approach for market queries; which standards apply; high-level technology direction; which OEM options to present in the proposal.

## Calculations

Order-of-magnitude/parametric only (e.g. approximate site count from route length) — enough to size a budgetary estimate, not a detailed link budget. Don't produce Stage 2/3-grade precision here; it isn't the point of this stage and can mislead the client's budget-setting.

## Deliverables

- Feasibility study report
- Stakeholder requirements register
- Initial high-level proposal, with OEM options
- Budgetary price estimate
- Project approval package (submitted to government/client/investor)

## Roles

- **Consultant** — producer of all deliverables at this stage
- **Client** — requester, and final approver of the package before it goes further to government/investor
- **OEM/Vendor** — responds to market queries with budgetary data
- **Contractor** — not engaged yet
